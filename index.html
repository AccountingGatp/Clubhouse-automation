Configuration

This is a single self-contained file — no separate config or rates file to host or upload. Business rules below (exclusions, tax/WC rates, entity keywords, thresholds) ship with sensible defaults built into the page; edits you make here are saved in this browser and used on future visits ("Export JSON" downloads a backup, "Import JSON" loads one back in — handy for moving your settings to another machine). Hourly rates are never entered here. Every employee's hourly rate comes only from the uploaded weekly payroll sheet's own "Hourly Rates" tab for that specific week — nothing else to maintain, and no old rate can be reused by mistake.

Job classifications (job-code based)

The app reads this straight from the timesheet, not from any name list: if any of an employee's job codes that week contains a keyword below, that classification applies to them for the week. Its workers' comp rate and its Clubhouse-exclusion flag are independent settings — a classification can give someone a special WC rate without excluding them from Clubhouse billing, or the reverse, purely based on what's configured here. Classified employees always show up in the NewClubhouse sheet and every audit sheet, so nothing is hidden even when excluded from billing.

Clubhouse billing exclusions

Per the SOP: employees whose pay for this period is calculated in a separate process and must not be billed here (currently Hazel Herrera and Paul Bates). This is the ONLY thing that removes an employee from the Clubhouse calculation — who's in scope otherwise is decided purely by the timesheet's own Jobcode1 data (anyone with Clubhouse/BluntD/Crysp hours that week), never by a roster or a job classification. This is a manual, name-based list, independent of job-code classification — a special workers' comp rate (below) does not by itself exclude anyone. Employees listed here are omitted from the NewClubhouse rows entirely (shown in a footnote instead) and never affect the subtotal.
