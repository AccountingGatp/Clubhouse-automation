Job classifications (job-code based)

The app reads this straight from the timesheet, not from any name list: if any of an employee's job codes that week contains a keyword below, that classification applies to them for the week. Its workers' comp rate and its Clubhouse-exclusion flag are independent settings — a classification can give someone a special WC rate without excluding them from Clubhouse billing, or the reverse, purely based on what's configured here. Classified employees always show up in the NewClubhouse sheet and every audit sheet, so nothing is hidden even when excluded from billing.

Clubhouse team roster

Per the team's SOP, this list — not each employee's computed Clubhouse ratio — decides who appears as a row in the NewClubhouse sheet each week. A roster member with 0% Clubhouse hours that week still gets a $0 row (for audit continuity); anyone not on this list never appears on the report, even if a job code happens to touch Clubhouse/BluntD/Crysp. Keep this list updated as the team changes. If left empty, the app falls back to automatic detection (billable ratio or a matching job classification) as a default.

Clubhouse billing exclusions

Roster members whose pay for this period is calculated in a separate process and must not be billed here (e.g. Hazel Herrera / Paul Bates / Elysia Castro per the current SOP). This is a manual, name-based list, independent of any job-code classification — a special workers' comp rate (below) does not by itself exclude anyone. Employees listed here are omitted from the NewClubhouse rows entirely (shown in a footnote instead) and never affect the subtotal.
